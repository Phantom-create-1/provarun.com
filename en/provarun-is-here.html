Provarun is here.

14 October 2026 · Paul Knutzen
Updated on 22 November 2026

Provarun has been on the App Store since 14 October. The app times sprints from 50 to 800 metres using video, displaying results to a hundredth of a second. It is free, requires an iPhone with iOS 26 or later, and is available in German in Germany, Austria, Switzerland and Luxembourg.

Why use video?

Over short distances, the timing method matters. Hetzler and colleagues compared handheld stopwatches with electronic timing. Errors occurred in both directions, making a reliable general correction factor impossible. [Hetzler et al., 2008]

Provarun measures time from the frames in your video. At a constant 60 frames per second, adjacent frames are about 17 milliseconds apart: one second divided by 60. You select the start and finish frame by frame and can check every result in the video.

How timing works

Set your phone beside the track, in line with the finish, using the same viewing principle as a finish-line camera. Set the countdown and walk to the start. Recording begins automatically. The German commands “Auf die Plätze” and “Fertig” are followed by a starting shot after a random delay, so you cannot anticipate it. Afterwards, move through the video frame by frame to find the moment you cross the finish line.

An iPhone is all you need, with no timing gates or Bluetooth accessories. You can also review footage you have already recorded by opening it from your photo library.

Your recordings stay on your device

No account. No registration. No server for your recordings. Videos, times and favourites stay exclusively on your iPhone. There are no ads, no tracking and no usage analytics. Track searches, maps, place names and journey times use the relevant external services. The optional Pro subscription also uses the App Store for purchases and restoring access.

Your own benchmark

Compare runs under similar conditions. Your saved videos show how each time was measured.
